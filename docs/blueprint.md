# **App Name**: GreenCover Connect

## Core Features:

- Rapid Claim Submission: Empowers golf course managers to quickly submit new claims from their mobile device, including photo uploads and essential details, with robust offline capabilities.
- Real-time Claim Status & Timeline: Provides users with an immediate overview of active claims, detailed status pages, a clear timeline of actions, and automatic progress notifications.
- Dedicated Manager Communication: Facilitates direct, trackable communication with the assigned Green Cover manager through an in-app chat and one-tap call functionality.
- Centralized Document Hub: Offers easy access to current policy details, coverage summaries, and all claim-specific documentation, with the ability to upload additional files.
- Green Cover Manager Back-office: A web-based platform built with Next.js for Green Cover managers to efficiently manage, update, and track all assigned claims, add internal notes, and administer user access.
- AI-powered Initial Damage Assessment: A generative AI tool for Green Cover managers that assists by analyzing submitted claim photos and descriptions to generate an initial summary of damage indicators.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Muted, professional forest green (#2E663D). Selected to evoke nature, golf courses, and the security of 'Green Cover', providing a calm and trustworthy foundation for the UI.
- Background color: Very light, almost off-white green (#EEF3F0). A desaturated and bright variant of the primary hue, ensuring a clean, spacious, and inviting canvas that enhances readability in a light color scheme.
- Accent color: Subtle, earthy olive green (#558C39). An analogous hue that provides visual interest and highlights interactive elements, distinct from the primary color while maintaining natural harmony.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans' (humanist sans-serif). Chosen for its excellent readability and balanced, approachable aesthetic, making the app simple and clear for all users, including non-technophiles.
- Minimalist and intuitive icons that clearly represent actions and claim statuses, reinforcing the app's promise of simplicity and ease of use. Icons should feature rounded corners and a uniform stroke weight.
- Mobile-first, clean, and guided layouts emphasizing direct navigation. Critical information such as claim status and manager contact is accessible within a minimal number of taps from the home screen.
- Subtle, quick transitions and micro-interactions that provide clear feedback to the user's actions without causing delays or distraction, reinforcing a sense of responsiveness and reliability.
